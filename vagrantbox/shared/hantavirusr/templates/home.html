Welcome to the Hantavirus Patient Database

This is a secure database for authorised medical staff to track and manage Hantavirus patients.

What is Hantavirus?

Hantavirus is a rare but serious illness transmitted to humans from infected rodents. Early tracking and monitoring of patients is critical for effective treatment.
